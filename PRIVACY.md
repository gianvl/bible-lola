# Privacy Policy — Bible (for Lola)

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

This app does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information.

- The app has **no internet access**: it does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
- There are **no ads, analytics, crash reporters, or accounts**.
- The only things the app remembers are your reading preferences: the chosen language, the text size, and the last chapter you read. These stay on your phone. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include them in your own device backup.
- "Read aloud" uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone. The app hands it the chapter text and nothing else.

If you have questions, please open an issue at https://github.com/gianvl/bible-lola/issues.
